BEFORE YOU ARRIVE.

Can I rent as an individual or a team?

Yes. Choose Individual Cage or Team Cage in the live booking form. The fielding area is a separate rental choice.

Do you offer baseball and softball training?

Yes. The facility supports both baseball and softball. Check the lane description and choose your sport when requesting a lesson.

When can I train?

Mon–Fri 4:00–8:00 PM reserved. Sat–Sun 1:00–8:00 PM by reservation. Special program times are listed with their registration.

How do lessons work?

Choose an instructor and preferred time. A lesson request becomes confirmed after Prospects approves the instructor, date, and time.

What is the cancellation policy?

The booking site lists a 24-hour cancellation notice requirement. Review the reservation or membership terms before booking.
