Straight answers

Can I walk in like a rec cage?

No — and that’s the point. Every hour is reserved, so the lane is yours. Book live availability, then show up ready to work.

$50 an hour looks high. Why not D-BAT?

Drop-in is the one-off rate. Two hours a month already beats Prospect math. Four hours a month is All-Star — about $35 an hour with priority booking.

Are you open during the day?

After school, on purpose. Monday–Friday 4–8 PM. Saturday–Sunday 1–8 PM by reservation. Youth athletes actually exist in that window.

Do you offer baseball and softball?

Yes. Lane 7 is dual-use for softball machine and baseball hitting. Choose your sport when you request a lesson.

What if I need to cancel?

24-hour notice is required. Review the reservation terms in checkout before you pay. Prefer card — the lane is held when it clears. Venmo and Cash App require you to come back and confirm.

Who to text

A real person answers. Desk for cages. Steve for lessons and member access.
