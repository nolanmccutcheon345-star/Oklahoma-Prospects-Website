# Independently funded teams

An unpublished team season can select **Independently Funded Team** from Front Office → Team Fees, or Account → Team finances. Existing published seasons and accepted organization agreements are not converted. The existing organization-managed model remains available.

Prospects billing reuses `team_fee_plans`, accepted player agreements, Square checkout, the existing payment/refund ledger, and the scheduled collection worker. The independent price branch uses only the admin-published initial deposit, finite monthly Prospects dues, and disclosed processing allowance. Drafts do not create obligations; publication does not authorize collection. Acceptance records the exact displayed schedule and policy, including a digest that rejects stale acceptance. Saved-card collection still requires the payer's separate existing authorization flow and the existing deployment verification gate.

Families and admins see **Initial deposit** on current fee schedules, new agreements, payment authorization and checkout. The underlying installment identity remains unchanged, and previously signed policy text stays intact. Admins have a private per-team deposit note plus cumulative actual uniform, coaching-pay and coach-travel cost inputs. These are team-season totals, not per-player costs. Saving this worksheet does not publish fees, rewrite accepted agreements, post expenses or move money. Notes, costs and profit calculations are excluded from coach, manager and family responses and independent team exports.

The admin profit worksheet distinguishes projected collections (accepted schedules plus published offers for current pending players), verified collections after refunds, credits, reserved overpayments, and actual known Square processing costs. Projected profit is before processing and other overhead; collected profit is based on applied receipts less entered costs and known Square fees, before external payment fees and other overhead. Missing cost inputs or processing fees and unreconciled receipts produce an incomplete result, not an assumed zero. Verified external Prospects receipts use the existing owner-verification evidence. Independently collected team funds and settlement transfers never become additional Prospects revenue.

Each independent obligation is identified by team, season UUID, player, and installment key. Agreement revisions do not replace that identity. Accrued and fully/partly paid installments retain their amounts; individual amendment offers replace only unaffected future rows. Acceptance archives the previous version and revokes old collection authority. The verified accepting guardian is the designated payer; an individual owner-published offer can designate a replacement verified guardian. Saved-card details remain private to the payer.

Late enrollment requires an explicit individual offer with reviewed dates and amounts. A transfer uses an explicit withdrawal from the former team's future Prospects billing and a separately accepted destination offer; balances and credits are retained in their original season. There is no guessed proration, automatic extra uniform deposit, or transfer of payment authorization. Separate team terms continue to govern independent operating charges.

Admin withdrawal or season cancellation voids future installments, retains amounts already due and documented committed uniform deposits within the accepted cap, and atomically revokes local automatic collection and ready/declined retries. Roster removal alone changes no contract. In the legacy storage mode, independent roster removals archive the player as inactive instead of deleting the financial identity; ordinary bulk roster saves cannot rewrite accepted fee or agreement fields. The active financial-basis mode already keeps the historical financial roster separately. Ordinary autopay revocation stops collection without forgiving the agreement. This integration creates individual Square payments, not provider subscriptions, so no Square recurring schedule exists to cancel. Already submitted payments are reconciled to their actual result. Verified payments dated after the cutoff trigger owner review; their money and any credit remain recorded rather than being hidden or automatically refunded. Pending reconciliation and payments recorded after the cutoff appear on the player's financial status. Vendor cost above an accepted deposit cannot create another player charge.

The team workspace stores independent activity in separate `independent_team_*` tables. Those tables are not organization revenue, cash, expense or profit sources. Prospects uniform spending remains in the existing organization Finance workflow, with separate organization-owned budget lines; reallocating that budget never increases player charges. No independent transaction or refund calls Square.

Only the currently assigned head coach receives full independent financial management automatically. Nolan, Steve and that head coach may appoint verified non-player financial managers with explicit capabilities. Managers cannot delegate or read Prospects balances merely through their appointment. Other assigned coaches retain read-only visibility into their team's Prospects status. Owners have read-only independent access unless a separate team assignment grants writes. Current account restrictions and assignment checks run on every request, receipt download and export, including for frozen financial records.

Posted independent entries and audits are append-only. Partial payments, explicit allocations, unapplied credits, linked credits, allocation corrections, externally completed refunds, and reversals have different effects. Payments and allocations count as one receipt. Spending commitments reduce available funds until replaced by actual expenses. Category allocation changes reclassify money without creating income. Planned refunds do not reduce cash. Balances say **Based on recorded transactions**, because the application does not verify the team's external bank balance.

Families receive only their own authorized player obligations and their own payment records. Shared reports expose category budgets, spending and commitments; they omit other families' receipts, incoming funds, individual transactions and private memos. An anonymous team payment-risk message is suppressed for small groups or when it would identify every other player as unpaid. Private receipt files are served only through authenticated requests. Downloads already obtained cannot be recalled. Individual reminders are private in-app records for a verified billing recipient and are deduplicated; this implementation does not send team-wide delinquency messages or activate an email reminder campaign.

Closeout uses open, closing and closed states and records funds, receivables, credits and commitments without automatically distributing or forgiving anything. A closed season blocks ordinary new entries and budget edits; an authorized reopening or correction records its reason. Completed external refunds require their original payment, amount, date, method and reference, and may settle a linked planned refund partially or fully. An explicit carryforward requires authority and published disposition terms in both source and destination seasons, reserves unapplied payer credits, and atomically creates matching transfer records without income. The operator distinguishes bookkeeping carryforward from an external cash transfer already completed. Funds available after commitments and funds available after reserving payer credits are labeled separately.

## Verification

- `src/lib/teams/independent.test.ts`: pricing isolation, exact finite schedules, acceptance, ledger calculations, scoped permissions, documents, immutable history and cancellation.
- Existing fee-server and collection regressions verify the organization-managed path and Square retry behavior.
- `scripts/check-independent-finances.mjs`: compiled application, isolated synthetic database, authenticated owner/coach/guardian/manager interactions and a 375px family viewport. No provider credentials, real charges or live notices are used.
- Required release gates remain typecheck, build, built SSR verification, the complete regression suite, lint, dependency audit and existing browser walkthroughs. Deployment is eligible only after the exact-source checks and Netlify preview pass.

Deploying this feature does not select a team's model, publish terms, accept agreements, enable autopay, import team funds or contact families. Those remain explicit authorized application actions.
