# Team pricing and Front Office financial audit — 2026-10-22

## Scope and evidence

Code and isolated synthetic-data audit of team fee composition, master pricing, season billing, roster projections, collection allocation, cash protection, compensation, customer receivables, Square payment/refund/settlement handling, rentals, subscriptions, discounts and merchandise checkout. No customer charges, bank transfers, fee publications, permission grants, or historical financial records were changed by the audit.

The reported estimate problem required tracing the combined team-cost line. The existing central fee engine already replaces scheduled/package costs with enabled admin estimates, including explicit zero. A synthetic regression reproduces the reported breakdown and reconciles every cent. The screenshot alone does not prove omitted estimates. The interface now exposes tournament totals, uniform price and baseline quantity, other direct costs, contingency and allocation, and distinguishes unsaved draft, saved draft and published fees. Accepted agreements remain immutable.

## Operational limits and remaining bookkeeping

This validates implemented calculation paths and regression scenarios, not every theoretical input or the completeness of real books. A certified organization balance still requires an authorized owner to supply/reconcile actual bank statements, complete Square historical imports, review missing provider fees/partial invoices, enter vendor/payroll/tax liabilities, and confirm earned profit. No missing cost or bank balance is invented. Paid expenses recorded in both team and Finance must refer to the same originating expense; unrelated manual duplicates require owner reconciliation.

Split-payment invoices retain the existing fail-closed refund-funding review when no single original payment can be safely allocated. This audit does not enable automatic transfers, bank connectivity, unapproved merchandise fulfillment, or automatic retries against backup cards. Existing customer obligations are not recalculated or republished by this software release.

Provider reference checked 2026-10-22: [Square invoice payment and refund behavior](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/invoices-api/pay-refund-invoices), including partial status and retrieval of payments through order tenders.
