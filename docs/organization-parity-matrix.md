## Existing product limits and withheld material

- Team documents are four checklist fields; Player Development documents are metadata. There is no general private file store to expose. Unclassified historical attachments, messages and narratives cannot safely be disclosed; owners may classify exact operational revisions or provide safe copies.
- No generic CMS or global nonfinancial portal-settings editor exists in the mounted owner portal. This release does not invent one.
- Operational event drafts use manual/office enrollment. Same-ID adoption into a priced event is not implemented; owners retain the existing priced Event Office creation flow. Services have an explicit owner adoption path.
- Guardian consent stays with the authorized guardian. Financial terms, billing access, owner/admin grants, recovery and credentials remain owner-controlled.
- Public published prices are public. Anonymous donor UUID receipts and external provider bearer links retain their existing behavior. Signed-in restricted users are denied the private financial routes; operational projections must not disclose receipt identifiers or URLs. This does not identify a person after they sign out.
- Account-level sticky financial restriction also excludes Sara's authenticated personal/household finance. Suspension/removal does not remove that restriction.

## Financial preservation

The user resolved the business rule: operational moves, contacts, coaching and schedules must not change existing billing responsibility, pay agreements or financial commitments. Stable financial agreement/team aliases support existing checkout and refund reconciliation. Captured billing principals and compensation attribution are independent from operational assignments. All actual financial readers and owner writers use the preserved basis after activation; provider ledgers stay live. New operations do not create monetary obligations until an owner explicitly adopts financial terms.

Capture, captured-source refresh and activation are separate, current-owner commands. Activation binds exact source/evidence/release/output digests, fixes the comparison date, validates historical bindings and pending attempts, and compares complete owner fee/finance/compensation projections in the same transaction. Any mismatch rolls back bindings, pointer and audit. Migrations create no capture, activation or access grant.
