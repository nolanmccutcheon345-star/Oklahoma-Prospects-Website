# Camps and clinics

Train → Camps lists published camps/clinics and a month calendar. Front Office → Camps & Clinics creates and edits name, sport, description, full-camp and/or per-day prices, location, one or more session dates/times (Central Time), coaches, capacity, publication status, and written registration/cancellation policy.

Admins choose full-camp only, individual days only, or both. Add a date for each camp day and edit its start/end independently; Copy first day’s times applies one time range to every date. A per-day purchase includes all sessions on each selected date; a full-camp purchase includes every listed session. Prices are per player. For example, a $125 package and $50 daily rate let families buy the whole camp for $125 or two days for $100. The selected dates, times, price, and policy are preserved on the purchase and receipt. There is no private-lesson assessment requirement. All event prices are read from admin records and validated again before Square payment. Event edits invalidate stale unpaid checkouts. Completed purchases retain their price and policy snapshot.

Pending checkout capacity lasts ten minutes. Registration is granted only through verified Square completion, once per purchased player/date. Capacity is enforced per day, including pending holds. A player can buy additional nonoverlapping camp days later. Full-camp registration closes when the first session starts; individual-day registration can remain open for future days. Payment amount, customer, environment, currency and location use the existing Square checks. Unavailable, expired or conflicting completions enter payment review and the existing compensating-refund queue; duplicate webhooks do not duplicate registrations. Full verified refunds cancel registrations and release player availability. Partial refunds retain the registration.

Published/closed events block assigned coaches’ lesson time. Registered players' sessions block their other bookings. No facility lanes are automatically allocated: admins must arrange sufficient event space. Once registered players exist, session dates, location and policy are locked; use Front Office payment review and replacement events for changes. Ordinary lesson rescheduling/cancellation cannot alter camp registrations. Close registration to stop new bookings; cancel only after active registrations have been resolved. No notification to families is sent just by editing an event; coordinate changes directly. Payment confirmations use the existing receipt queue.

Migration 0047 creates event and registration records. Migration 0049 permits separate purchases of disjoint dates for the same player; duplicate dates and capacity remain protected by a database event-row lock. Public endpoints omit participants and private coach data. Admin editing requires verified owner grants; registration requires guardian household billing access. Production verification does not submit real charges or publish invented events.

Admins can optionally set youngest and oldest player ages (inclusive, in years). Eligibility is based on the first camp date for both full-camp and selected-day purchases. Missing/invalid birthdays and ages outside the configured limits block checkout and are rechecked before payment and fulfillment. Existing unrestricted events stay unrestricted. Birthdays are returned only for the signed-in billing household's players, never in public event data. Purchased eligibility limits are stored with the order; existing registrations are not cancelled when an admin edits limits.

Event types: Camp, Clinic, Skills class, Small-group training, Speed & agility session, Showcase, Player combine, Open practice, and Special event. These share the existing paid event registration flow.
