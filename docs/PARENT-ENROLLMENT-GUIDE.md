# Connected parent enrollment

Parents start at `/account`, accept any pending household invitation, then use **Complete your player’s enrollment**. Each player links to `/enrollment?teamId=…&playerId=…`. The existing document packet, medical form, annual waiver, fee acceptance, agreement download, Square checkout and two-card installment authorization remain authoritative. Team payment confirmations link back to that player’s enrollment; `/account?desk=team-finances` supports both funding models. Independently assigned financial managers retain their team workspace even without a Prospects billing or roster role; this account view loads those grants separately from Prospects fee-plan visibility.

The guide reads current account/household authority and financial admission separately. Roster access cannot admit a new financial identity. Missing published fees, enrollment activation, document publication or financial admission is shown as waiting for Prospects. Fee acceptance requires the current guardian’s packet signature. Completing parent steps does not grant participation clearance. Optional media consent is not a required step.

Progress never writes acceptance, payment or authorization records. Deposit completion requires reconciled production provider receipts, verified offline evidence and permitted credits through the existing enrollment finance projection. Pending/unknown attempts remain unresolved. Card storage is separate from schedule authorization; global checkout/collection safeguards remain enforced. The guide refreshes after mutations, on return/focus and every 30 seconds while visible. Payment history and receipts remain in the family workspace.

## Fees-ready delivery

Migration `0115_fee_ready_notifications.sql` creates an empty outbox; it contains no historical backfill. New publication, individual independent offer, household invitation/acceptance, newly added roster player and explicit financial admission events queue eligible published offers atomically with the action. Nothing is sent from a database transaction. Existing accepted fees are excluded unless a specific pending individual amendment requires review.

The scheduled `/api/tryout-notifications` worker dispatches at most two fee notices per invocation. Its existing production scheduler and `TRYOUT_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED`, `TRYOUT_NOTIFICATIONS_SECRET`, Resend configuration and invitation email pause control apply. Previews retain the existing explicit test-recipient allowlist. This change does not enable any production flag.

Recipient eligibility is checked again before dispatch: current invite/household authority, current operational assignment, authorized financial identity, individual payer, published terms, cancellations and account restrictions. Deduplication uses player, team, immutable season identity, recipient and public offer content. Unchanged publication, draft edits, display-name changes and retries do not create another notice. The exact original request body and idempotency key are reused; uncertain retries stop before the provider’s idempotency window expires. Expired/revoked/superseded notices are retained as suppressed history.

Owners use **Team Fees → Parent fee emails — delivery and catch-up preview** to inspect recent delivery status and preview exact eligible catch-up messages. Selecting and queuing a reviewed preview is an explicit send request; changed recipients or offers require another preview. Provider acceptance is not delivery. The provider status check reports delivered, bounced, delayed or failed where available. Neither this preview nor loading the guide sends mail.

## Release verification and limits

Synthetic regression coverage includes both invitation/publication orders, exact fee explanation and amounts, verification/guardian boundaries, packet gating, accepted snapshots, resumable acceptance, idempotent payment preparation, unknown payment status, immutable email retry payloads, deduplication, historical catch-up preview, stale suppression, late roster additions and restricted staff. Existing provider-mock suites cover verified collection, partial payments, primary/backup declines, uncertain outcomes, revocation and no repeat deposit collection. The compiled enrollment browser walkthrough covers invitations, the account guide, player selection, actual packet and fee acceptance, deposit amount, paused autopay, saved progress, owner email status, mobile layout and medical privacy.

Passing synthetic tests is not proof of real inbox delivery, a real card charge, provider sandbox certification or production autopay readiness. Do not send real parent messages, use customer accounts, charge payments, authorize on a family’s behalf or enable payment flags for testing. Check non-secret readiness through the owner controls. Any provider sandbox exercise requires its own authorized sandbox environment. Record exact source, CI, preview isolation and production verification in the release PR.
