## Operational policy decisions

- Steve is the primary safety contact and Nolan the alternate, using their existing owner contact addresses. Reports involving an owner route to the other owner; external reporting and emergency services bypass internal escalation. Contact configuration does not claim continuous monitoring or a test delivery.
- Goals are established within 30 days; each athlete receives at least one documented in-season update and a final evaluation, or a higher published team minimum. Baseline assessment remains recommended, and actual assessment completion is never inferred from a checklist.
- Planned absence notice is 48 hours for practices and 14 days for tournaments. Emergencies, medical restrictions and late schedule changes are communicated as soon as reasonably possible without automatic discipline.
- Seasonal withdrawals follow the signed season agreement. Organization cancellation receives a written, itemized determination within 30 days. Replacement adjustments cannot recover the same expense twice. Cage, lesson and membership terms remain separate.
- Actual owner-published team fees, overrides, installment dates, the 70/15/15 schedule and program-fee treatment outside contingency remain unchanged. This release sets no dollar amount and changes no financial formula.
- Safeguarding, concussion response, venue emergency preparation, weather, supervision and optional media rules are published as operational requirements. Organization activation does not certify actual staff training, venue readiness, insurance coverage or professional legal approval.

## Participation and records

The guardian and athlete actively acknowledge concussion information annually; the immutable record retains names, materials, statement and date. Expired and future-dated records do not satisfy the current requirement. Only the guardian/owners see signed personal records; staff receive the permitted completion status. Existing packet and financial signatures remain intact after revisions.

Owner clearance still requires a linked athlete, current waiver and concussion acknowledgment, current packet, medical information, accepted fee agreement and sufficient reconciled production Square funds or approved credits. The owner also attests to the actual staff safety/training and venue preparations before clearance. Sandbox, pending, unreconciled or different-agreement payments cannot qualify. Refund behavior and medical-change invalidation remain enforced. Optional media never blocks enrollment or enables public roster/recruiting publication.
