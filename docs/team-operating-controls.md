# Team operating controls

This release implements owner-directed operating rules without publishing team fees, changing signed agreements, charging cards, sending invitations or asserting legal/insurance approval. The prior technical audit remains the baseline; only changed paths and mandatory release regressions are retested.

## Controls

- Finance → Team spending is owner-only using the existing financial authority resolver. No coordinator or coach permission is added. Owners certify current team-only settled cash with masked evidence, final-roster deposit verification and protection of existing commitments. At least nine recorded accepted/deposit-covered players and deposits from every final-roster player are required. Recorded coverage is a prerequisite, not proof of settlement; the explicit owner review supplies that evidence.
- Publishing fees pins the team's original spending budget and publication version. Every custom cost, head/assistant coach allocation, tournament, uniform, scheduled hotel, other per-player cost and contingency line has its own ledger. Later draft/roster/default changes or fee republication do not rewrite it. Program and organization allocations, processing allowance and rounding are displayed separately from external spending, avoiding duplicate expenses.
- Each line shows original/current budget, requested/allocated payables, approved amounts, bank-reconciled paid amounts, payments sent awaiting reconciliation, historical unverified payments and remaining unallocated budget. Expand a line for its payees, invoice references, due dates and payment history; export a dated team/version CSV. Refresh source accruals to import existing expenses; classify unassigned and historical paid entries before new approvals. A historical paid marker is not bank verification.
- New expense approvals require today's review. Roster/accepted terms/payments/bank evidence or audited allocation changes invalidate it. Approvals consume reviewed cash in the same revision-checked transaction; bulk failures roll back. Any overallocated line blocks new approvals. Owners may allocate only unused contingency into a line, or return an unused prior contingency allocation; original amounts and the total team ceiling remain unchanged. Every adjustment/reclassification retains an audit record. Internal program allocation is excluded from the external spending ceiling.
- Shared invoices use one payable/payment allocation per team, a common vendor/invoice reference, and distinct payment references (for example, a check number plus team). Each team independently passes its budget and funding checks. Reconcile those allocations together only when their exact sum equals one matching bank debit; replay, mismatched totals/accounts/vendors and unauthorized actions are rejected atomically. Recording or matching does not send money.
- The older Team Fees actual-expense summary includes Finance payables and deduplicates linked fee-plan expenses. Unverified history and sent payments remain separate. Cash-protection allocation follows audited contingency transfers and expense reclassification. Season close rejects unpaid, unassigned, overbudget or unreconciled Finance expenses even when the fee-plan expense list is empty; historical paid checkboxes alone cannot close a season. Use existing generated coach/stipend payables for their partial payments instead of duplicating the same obligation.
- Reviewed cash cannot exceed that team's recorded fee/fundraising funding, or aggregate reconciled cash after organization reserve/tax/restricted protections. Actual source attribution and all existing commitment deductions are owner-certified; this is not an automated bank feed, full fund-accounting system, or permission to use another team's funds. Refresh the review before each external payment. The site does not execute outgoing transfers. Truthful records of already-sent payments must remain possible.
- Expense-plan editing can newly mark an expense paid or increase a paid amount only when the matching Finance payable has already been bank-reconciled for at least that amount. Use Finance payment recording and bank matching first. Existing historical expense markers remain historical evidence, not new verified bank transactions.
- Finance → Fundraising credits applies a linked completed contribution to the exact accepted athlete agreement only after production Square settlement entries match a reconciled bank receipt. Credit is limited to net settled proceeds after provider fees/refunds and unpaid fee balance. It is separate from cash receipts and idempotent. No new credit is applied to a withdrawn player or closed team. Excess proceeds remain unapplied for owner disposition under fundraiser/donor terms.
- Subsequent verified fundraising payment/refund or dispute reconciliation can only reduce an existing applied credit. Disputes are version-ordered; only a confirmed win makes proceeds eligible again, and reapplication still requires owner action. It preserves original fee terms, revokes enrollment clearance for review and pauses automatic collection after a reduction. Refreshing provider data remains operationally required; a webhook configuration or database test is not evidence of a live Square transaction.
- New discretionary uniform waivers and arbitrary credits through club editing are rejected. Historical credits remain. The explicit owner fundraising workflow replaces unverified concessions.
- Assigned coaches and financial administrators receive a private overdue list with athlete and listed guardian names, without balances, payment instruments or transaction details. Parents receive only a team-level overdue boolean and tracking state: no names, individual statuses or overdue-player count. Unassigned coaches, players and outsiders cannot access this endpoint. Other operational permissions are unchanged.
- Family, staff and owner operating guides are available in team fee workspaces. One-card families may securely save a primary card and pay manually by each due date. Automatic installment activation still requires two different cards and separate consent. This release does not weaken the two-decline requirement for the existing 20% late-fee implementation.

## Procedures and limits

Offer expiration and waitlists, the 72-hour late-entry full-payment deadline, facility bookings, vendor paperwork, uniform fulfillment, weekly 13-week cash forecasting, month-end accounting and incident staffing are documented owner/staff procedures. They are not represented as newly automated. Front Office remains the system of record; evidence and dated close packages must be retained. Refund payment reversal is distinct from a fee reduction; write-offs are distinct from releasing a contractual obligation.

No discretionary hardship/extension/late-fee-waiver program is offered. Correcting an error, unauthorized charge or provider/site-caused failure is an accounting correction, not a waiver. Voluntary withdrawal differs from a team that never forms or an undelivered season. Mandatory remedies, donor restrictions and provider dispute outcomes remain recorded even after season close. Prospective contract clauses need applicable legal review; no automated approval or fabricated adviser confirmation is added.

No live financial values, guardian records or bank references belong in this public repository. The owner operating package and team-specific readiness decisions are maintained privately.
