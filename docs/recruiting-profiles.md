# Recruiting profiles

Teams → Players (`/players`) contains only profiles with recorded guardian consent. Each profile at `/player/:id` uses the existing brand. `/player-profiles` is the authenticated editor and verification inbox, linked from Front Office Teams & Players / Evaluations, Coach Desk, Family, and the directory. The earlier `/recruiting` information page remains available.

A family player record (`club_athletes.id`) is the stable recruiting identity. Parents edit household-linked players; players edit only their administrator-linked identity. Admins may edit drafts but cannot authorize public visibility for another family. The guardian explicitly signs public-profile consent; withdrawing it removes the profile and stats from both public endpoints. Draft creation does not publish. Photos are resized in the browser; birthdays, financial/medical data, guardian contact details, private feedback, and evidence URLs never enter the public projection. Public biographies must not contain private details.

Front Office → Teams & Players → Recruiting profiles → select a player → Link team records connects the precise roster record to that family player. No name-based merging. Links establish which team stats belong to a person and which current head coach may verify metrics. Multiple team links retain history. The existing Staff & Access player assignment can link a player's login by the canonical player ID or a linked roster ID.

Players/parents enter measured values and dates, optionally requesting verification. Requests appear in the admin, assigned head coach, and assigned lesson instructor approval inbox. A team assistant role alone, unrelated coaches/families, and the player cannot approve. Approval requires an explicit verification method, stores the verifier/date, and uses revision checks. Editing or resubmitting a value always clears verification. Rejected values remain unverified; feedback is private. Audit records preserve submissions and review actions.

Profiles pull final game counts from the same `team_activities` records used by Games and the team workspace. Team staff can select each game's season. The default profile filter is the current calendar season in Central Time; visitors can select a team, another season, or all seasons. Legacy totals are reconciled against per-game counts to prevent double counting. Where a multi-season team's old totals lack a season, they remain Unassigned season; staff must not invent a historical allocation. Additional recorded rates and other stats remain separate per team/season, because averaging rates would be misleading. Batting average is recalculated from total hits and at-bats.

Migration 0050 adds recruiting profiles, exact roster links, and revision-controlled metrics. No existing records are automatically published. No real consent, registration, email, or production player data is created during deployment verification.

## Lesson instructor measurements

Migration 0051 records the source lesson for instructor-entered verified measurements. An active instructor with a confirmed or completed lesson assignment can review that player's metric requests. Instructor permission is derived from actual booking coach IDs, not a general instructor flag or arbitrary player selection. Cancelled/held bookings and camp/cage orders do not grant access.

During or after an assigned lesson, Record verified player metric is available in Paid Sessions, the guided lesson, and Recruiting workspace → Lesson players. The instructor enters the observed value and verification method. Save immediately updates the recruiting measurement as Verified using the lesson's Central Time date, records the instructor and source booking, and audits the change. It does not wait for lesson completion or another approval. A future lesson may establish the student relationship, but cannot be used to record a measurement before its start. Admins can record against valid lessons as well.

New profile records created by lesson entries remain private drafts. Existing guardian consent controls public visibility. Instructor access does not grant biography editing, birthday access, or publication rights. A later player/parent edit clears verification and the source-lesson reference; stale updates and stale approvals are rejected.
