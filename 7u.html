The Spring 2027 package

  • Indian Springs league play. League acceptance and game schedule to be confirmed.
  • Four tournaments. Events and dates selected with the coach after schedules are available.
  • One 90-minute indoor team practice per week, January–May; 22 sessions planned. Practice day and time to be confirmed.
  • Uniform package: two jerseys, two pants, and one hat.
  • Player insurance and team registration.
  • One free 30-minute cage reservation per player each week during the season, subject to availability.
  • 10% off lessons during the season.
  • Team baseballs and club administration.

Outdoor practice-field rentals, family travel, spectator admission, and personal equipment are separate.
